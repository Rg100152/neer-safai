National missions for safe water and sanitation.

From rural tap connections to river rejuvenation, these flagship programmes form the backbone of India's water security strategy.

How to avail benefits

Four simple steps.

Most schemes are delivered through your Gram Panchayat or Urban Local Body. Keep your identity and address documents ready.

Download application forms
  1. STEP 01

    Check eligibility

    Confirm your area is covered under the relevant mission.

  2. STEP 02

    Collect documents

    Aadhaar, address proof and recent water bill if applicable.

  3. STEP 03

    Submit application

    Apply at your Panchayat, ULB office or online portal.

  4. STEP 04

    Track & verify

    Use the reference number to follow status and inspections.

Facing a water issue in a covered area?

File a complaint and the concerned mission officer will review it through the Neer Safai dashboard.
