The bigger picture

Conservation is a civic duty.

India has 18% of the world's population but only 4% of its freshwater resources. Household-level conservation directly reduces pressure on rivers, groundwater and energy used for pumping and treatment.

  • Every litre saved also saves the electricity used to treat and pump it.
  • Rooftop harvesting can recharge local groundwater significantly.
  • Reporting public leaks is as important as saving water at home.
