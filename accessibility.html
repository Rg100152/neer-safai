Accessible to every citizen.

Public services must work for everyone, including persons with disabilities, senior citizens and users on slow connections.

Our commitment

Neer Safai aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and the principles of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. Accessibility is treated as a core requirement, not an afterthought.

What we have implemented

  • Semantic HTML landmarks and headings
  • Keyboard-operable menus, forms and modals
  • Visible labels and inline validation messages
  • High-contrast text on dark backgrounds
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences
  • Responsive layout for small screens
  • Descriptive alt text on key images
  • ARIA labels on interactive controls

Known limitations

Some decorative animations and third-party CDN components may not fully meet AA contrast targets in every browser. Automated screen-reader testing has not yet been completed for this academic build. We are actively improving these areas.
