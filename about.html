A digital backbone for water sanitation in India.

Neer Safai is a demonstration civic-tech platform built to model how citizens and government can collaborate on water quality and sanitation accountability.

Our Mission

Ensure every citizen has a simple, transparent channel to report water issues and receive a verifiable resolution from the responsible authority.

Our Vision

A future where water quality data is open, actionable and available in real time — driving smarter policy and healthier communities.

Our Values

Accountability, accessibility, data integrity and citizen-first service delivery guide every feature we build.

Mandate

From grievance to governance.
