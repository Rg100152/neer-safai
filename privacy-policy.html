Neer Safai ("the Portal") is a demonstration civic-tech application. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Portal and how it is handled. In a production deployment, this policy would be issued by the operating department under applicable Indian law.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact data: name and 10-digit mobile number submitted with a complaint.
  • Location data: state, district or city, and the pincode entered in the quality checker.
  • Complaint content: issue type, description and, optionally, a photo filename.
  • Feedback data: optional name, email, role, rating and message.

2. How we use information

Information is used solely to register, route, track and resolve water sanitation grievances, and to generate aggregated analytics for service improvement. We do not use personal data for advertising or profiling.

3. Data storage

In this demo build, complaints and feedback are stored in your browser's localStorage. If the optional Python Flask backend is running, complaints are additionally stored in a local SQLite database (database.db). No data is transmitted to third-party servers.

4. Sharing of information

Complaint details are visible to authorised officials through the admin dashboard for the purpose of resolution. We do not sell or rent personal data. Aggregated, non-identifiable statistics may be published for public transparency.

5. Security

Access to the admin dashboard requires a session stored in localStorage. Production deployments must use HTTPS, hashed credentials, server-side sessions, rate limiting and audit logging. This demo is not suitable for storing real sensitive personal data.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your complaint data by contacting the grievance officer. In the demo build, you can clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site.
